Productivity Guidelines

Best practices for efficient study
The 25-5 Pomodoro Rule

Study for 25 minutes with full focus, then take a 5-minute break to retain more knowledge.

Prioritize High Urgency

Tackle High priority topics earlier in your week when focus levels are highest.

Bite-sized Milestones
